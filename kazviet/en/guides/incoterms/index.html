Incoterms 2020 for Kazakhstan–Vietnam trade and shipping

EXW, FOB, CIF and DAP allocate delivery obligations, costs and risk between buyer and seller. The relevant rule depends on the actual transaction.

Preparing the transaction

Prepare each enquiry for the actual product and corridor. Match parties' records, product specifications, shipping documents and allocation of risks.

  1. Define the transport mode and handover point.
  2. Select the applicable Incoterms 2020 rule.
  3. State the named place and rules version in the contract.
  4. Agree on payment, documentation and insurance separately.
The International Chamber of Commerce publishes the official Incoterms 2020 guidance. Official source ↗

Information to include in an enquiry

Provide product specifications, order quantity, destination and buyer contact information.
