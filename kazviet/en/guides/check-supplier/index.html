How to verify a Vietnam supplier before wholesale orders

Practical due diligence for company registration, payment details, product documents and inspection.

Preparing the transaction

Prepare each enquiry for the actual product and corridor. Match parties' records, product specifications, shipping documents and allocation of risks.

  1. Match the legal entity name, registration and banking details.
  2. Check that the contract counterparty matches the payment beneficiary.
  3. Agree on samples, specifications and acceptance conditions.
  4. Set out quality checks and shipment evidence.

Information to include in an enquiry

Provide product specifications, order quantity, destination and buyer contact information.
