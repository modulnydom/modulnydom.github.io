Preparing the transaction

Prepare each enquiry for the actual product and corridor. Match parties' records, product specifications, shipping documents and allocation of risks.

  1. Provide origin, destination, commodity type and gross weight.
  2. Check dimensions, container choice and packing requirements.
  3. Compare applicable multimodal corridors and transfers.
  4. Define insurance, transport documents and clearance requirements.
